ROBOTICS & AI ENGINEER PARIS, FRANCE

Riccardo Mengozzi.

Hi, I’m Riccardo. I’m a robotics engineer in Paris, interested in how robots can learn to handle objects whose shape, position, or behavior changes as they move them.

I studied automation engineering at the University of Bologna and spent a semester at NTNU in Trondheim. At Bologna’s Automation and Robotics Lab, I developed diffusion policies for reshaping flexible objects like cables. I built simulation and synthetic data pipelines and tested learned policies on a physical UR5.

I now work at Wandercraft on humanoid manipulation. My work spans the imitation learning pipeline, from collecting human demonstrations to training policies and deploying them on Calvin.
